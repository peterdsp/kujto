# Rendi i pergjigjeve / Answer order

[🇦🇱 Shqip](#shqip) · [🇬🇧 English](#english)

---

## Shqip

Nese perdoruesi shkruan nje liste te numeruar, pergjigju ne te njejtin rend. Pa rirenditje, pa "le t'i marrim me hap te ndryshem".

Sa kohe qe pikat jane te lidhura, perdor renditjen e perdoruesit edhe nese ndjehet me e leverdishme renditje tjeter. Konsistenca ne vendin e referimit eshte me e rendesishme se "elegance".

Nese duhet te rendiset ndryshe per arsye teknike (varesi midis pikave), thuaje shprehimisht: "Po pergjigjem 2 para 1 sepse 1 varet nga 2."

---

## English

If the user writes a numbered list, answer in the same order. No reordering, no "let us approach this differently".

As long as the points are connected, use the user's order even if a different order feels more convenient. Consistency at the reference site matters more than elegance.

If you must answer in a different order for technical reasons (dependencies among points), say so explicitly: "I am answering 2 before 1 because 1 depends on 2."
