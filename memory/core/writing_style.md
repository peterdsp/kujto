# Stili i shkrimit / Writing style

[🇦🇱 Shqip](#shqip) · [🇬🇧 English](#english)

---

## Shqip

### Rregulli i forte
Pa vize te gjate (`—`) ne asnje dalje. Asnje prozme, koment kodi, mesazh commit-i, koment PR ose dokumentacion. Perdor presje, pike, kllapa, dy pika, ose vize te zakonshme (`-`).

Pse: ky eshte rregull identiteti i projektit dhe shenjon dalje te shkruajtura nga njeriu kunder atyre te paevitueshme AI. CI-ja do ta refuzoje vizen e gjate.

### Toni
- Natyror, direkt, pa zbukurime te tepruara.
- Pa "delight", "robust", "leverage" te boshatisura.
- Mesazhet e commit-it ne kohen e tashme imperative (`shto`, `rregullo`, `hiq`).
- Lista te shkurtra, jo paragrafe te gjate, kur stafi ka bullet-list ne mendje.

### Struktura
- Headers te qarte, line breaks te shumta.
- Kod te mbeshtetet ne ``` me gjuhen e specifikuar.
- Linke me tekst kuptimplote, jo "click here".

## English

### Hard rule
No em-dash (`—`) in any output. Not in prose, code comments, commit messages, PR comments, or documentation. Use commas, periods, parens, colons, or a regular hyphen (`-`).

Why: this is the project's identity rule and it distinguishes human-written output from unavoidably AI-flavoured output. CI will reject the em-dash.

### Tone
- Natural, direct, no padding.
- No empty "delight", "robust", "leverage".
- Commit messages in imperative present (`add`, `fix`, `remove`).
- Short lists, not long paragraphs, when the reader expects a bullet list.

### Structure
- Clear headers, generous line breaks.
- Code in fenced blocks with the language specified.
- Links with meaningful anchor text, no "click here".
